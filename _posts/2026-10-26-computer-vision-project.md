I'm just wrapping up a fun and challenging consulting project in image processing and computer vision. A big part of the fun was the team: I enjoyed working with them, and teaching the methods underneath the work as we went.

I came to computer vision by way of computer graphics, and the two are the same problem run in opposite directions: graphics turns a description of a world into pixels, and vision turns pixels back into a description. A lot of the best ideas cross over. Graphics engines learned long ago not to spend effort evenly, drawing what is near in fine detail and what is far coarsely, and the same instinct pays off in vision: put the computation where the information is.
