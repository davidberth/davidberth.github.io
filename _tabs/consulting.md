## How I work

I don't just implement solutions; I enable your team to carry them forward. Alongside the code, I teach the methods underneath it, so your team understands the system well enough to run it, question it, and keep building on it. The result is a working approach your team owns.

## Where I'm strongest

- **Geospatial.** Large-scale analysis of imagery and spatial data, from raw rasters and vectors to measurements a team can validate and build on.
- **Computer vision.** Detecting, segmenting, and measuring what's in an image, from classical methods to deep learning, validated against ground truth.
- **Computer graphics.** Real-time rendering, GPU programming, and procedural generation, the work behind *The Dimming*.
- **Applied AI.** Designing and shipping ML and generative systems for problems that don't fit off-the-shelf tooling, including real-time inference, reinforcement learning, and small LLMs running under tight latency and compute budgets.
- **Scientific computing.** Numerical methods, simulation, and large-scale compute on heterogeneous hardware (CPU and GPU).
- **Research engineering.** Taking a paper, prototype, or internal research effort and producing a validated, well-engineered implementation.
- **Algorithm design.** Graph-theoretic, combinatorial, and partition-based algorithms, with computational backing where it matters.
